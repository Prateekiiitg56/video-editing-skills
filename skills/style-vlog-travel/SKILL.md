# Style: Vlog / Travel

Load with: `editing-foundations`, `beat-sync`, `color-grading`, `sound-design`, `captions-typography`.

## Signature

- Journey arc: departure/arrival → exploration → highlight → quiet moment → departure/reflection.
- Mix of: establishing wides, details (food, hands, textures), people, POV walks, drone/top-downs, time-lapses.
- Transitions hidden in motion: whip pans, objects passing the lens (mask transitions), match cuts (door → door, circle → circle), hand covers lens.
- Ambient sound for each place (market noise, waves) under the music; a few seconds of sound-only moments.
- Location titles: small, clean, with place name + optional coordinates/date; map animation for route.
- Vlogs: talking-to-camera bits with jump cuts and captions; B-roll covers narration.

## Recipe

1. Log footage by location and type (wide/detail/people/POV/transition-able motion).
2. Pick music (or 2 tracks for two moods); detect beats.
3. Open with the most striking shot or a fast 3-5 s montage teaser, then title.
4. Per location: wide → 2-4 details → people/action → a transition shot to the next location.
5. Transitions: plan pairs (end of shot A has a whip right, start of shot B has a whip right) and cut in the middle of the blur, 4-8 frames each side.
6. Speed: hyperlapse/time-lapse for travel between places; slow-mo for one or two hero moments.
7. Grade: warm-golden for sunny/nostalgic, clean neutral-punch for city, teal-orange for adventure; match all cameras (phone + action cam + drone).
8. Titles and map; captions for talking parts.
9. End on a reflective wide shot or a loop back to the first shot.

## Map / route animation

Draw the route as a path that grows with ease-in-out over 2-4 s on a stylised map (static map image + PIL/HTML path), dot at the destination pulses on arrival; SFX whoosh + soft pop.

## Quality bar

Horizon level on wides; no shaky walking shots without stabilization; every place recognisable from its establishing shot; ambience fades (≥ 0.3 s) between locations.
