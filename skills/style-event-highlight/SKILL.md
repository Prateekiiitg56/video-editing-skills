# Style: Event highlight

Load with: `editing-foundations`, `beat-sync`, `sound-design`, `color-grading`, `captions-typography`.

## Signature

- Arc: anticipation (prep, venue, details) → arrival/entrance → key moments (vows, stage, goal) → celebration peak → quiet farewell.
- Audio storytelling: best lines from speeches/vows/hosts laid over B-roll (L-cuts), music ducked under them, then music up.
- Slow motion for emotional peaks (50-60 fps sources at 40-50%).
- Detail shots (rings, decor, food, tickets, banners) as cut points between sections.
- Titles: names + date + place, elegant serif/script for weddings, bold sans for events/sports.

## Versions to deliver

| Version | Length | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Teaser | 30-60 s, 9:16 | social |
| Highlight | 3-6 min, 16:9 | YouTube/gift |
| Full ceremony/talks | full length, light edit | archive |

## Recipe

1. Log moments; mark audio gold (laughs, cheers, vows, best quotes).
2. Pick 1-2 songs; map sections (prep = intro, entrance = build, celebration = chorus).
3. Lay the audio story (speech lines) first, then music around it.
4. Cover with visuals; reactions after key lines (L-cut).
5. Sports: goal/point moments with replays at 50% speed, crowd reaction, scoreboard text; keep play direction readable.
6. Grade warm and flattering for people events (protect skin); punchy for sports/concerts (handle stage lights: don't clip LED colours).
7. End on a wide shot, the couple/team/crowd, with title and date.

## Quality bar

Every face shown in a key moment is flattering and in focus; speech lines are clean and intelligible over music; names and dates are spelled exactly as given.
