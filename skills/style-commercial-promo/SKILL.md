# Style: Commercial / Promo

Load with: `editing-foundations`, `color-grading`, `captions-typography`, `beat-sync`, `sound-design` (+ `reference-matching` when the client gives a reference).

## Signature

- Hook in the first second: best product/food/space shot or the offer as text.
- Structure: **Hook → Problem/desire → Product/place → Benefits/proof (3 beats) → Offer → CTA**.
- Hero shots: slow push-ins (100 → 108% over the shot, ease-in-out), macro details, steam/pour/sizzle moments, people enjoying.
- Brand-consistent type, colours and logo; premium = more space, fewer words.
- End card: logo + CTA (Follow / Order / Visit / Link in bio) + location only if verified, held 2-3 s.

## Sub-genres

| Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Restaurant / food | warm-golden grade, quick cuts 1.3-1.7 s, slow push-ins, steam and texture close-ups, script or serif captions, gold/cream type on dark; end card over a blurred dark frame |
| Premium announcement | dark brand-colour background, title card, framed photo with translucent text band, subtle gold bokeh drifting, xfade transitions, like/share/follow icons on end card |
| Product (e-commerce) | clean white or brand background, 360° turn, feature callouts with lines pointing to parts, UGC-style hands-on shots |
| Real estate | wide establishing, smooth gimbal walkthrough in room order, 0.8-1x speed, bright neutral grade (lift shadows, keep windows not clipped), room labels |
| Paid social ad | 6/15/30 s versions; subtitles always; the brand appears in the first 3 s; one CTA |

## Recipe

1. Shot selection: only the best clips — drop shaky, selfie-angle, cluttered, menu-board-only and watermarked shots. Crop phone watermarks out or skip the clip.
2. Mix sources if the client has old + new footage: interleave so new content dominates but variety stays.
3. Beat-sync cuts and text reveals to the client's music (librosa beats).
4. Per shot: crop to the format, slow zoom, grade (`warm-golden` for food, `neutral-punch` for products).
5. Text: name/title with type-on or mask reveal; short captions (3-6 words) per benefit; script + serif pairing for elegant, bold sans for offers.
6. End card: logo, CTA, verified contact/location only.
7. Exports: 9:16 for reels/stories, 4:5 for feed ads, 1:1 if requested, 16:9 for YouTube/web.

## Hard rules

- **Never invent business facts** (prices, phone numbers, addresses, opening hours, awards). Use only what the client provided; otherwise use something generic ("Near X station") the user confirms.
- Check spelling of the business name on the real signage.
- Music must be licensed for commercial use, or added in-app by the client.

## Quality bar

Food looks appetising (warm, not orange; whites still white), product edges sharp, logo crisp at final size, CTA readable for ≥ 2 s, nothing important in the platform UI zones.
