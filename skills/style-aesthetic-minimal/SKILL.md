# Style: Aesthetic / Minimal

Load with: `color-grading`, `captions-typography`, `sound-design`, `beat-sync` (light).

## Signature

- Slow: 2-4 s shots, few camera moves (slow push or static), no zoom punches or flashes.
- Composition: top-down desk, symmetry, negative space, soft window light, practical lamps at night.
- Grade: `pastel-matte` (lifted blacks, low contrast) or `warm-golden`/`film-kodak` at low strength; grain 4-6%; gentle vignette.
- Text: lowercase, short, quiet ("one more commit, then sleep."); italic serif accent in a soft colour (peach, cream) + small sans; or no text.
- Sound: foley up front (keyboard, pages, cup on table, rain), lo-fi/piano/ambient bed; cut on soft beats, not every beat.
- Transitions: straight cuts or 0.3-0.6 s dissolves; occasional match cut (cup → cup).

## Recipe

1. Select calm, well-composed shots; drop anything shaky.
2. Order by time of day or by a small story (start → focus → break → night).
3. Cut on every 2nd or 4th beat; let a few shots run long.
4. Grade all clips the same; add grain after scaling.
5. Text: 1-3 lines total, inside the centre; 0.5-0.8 s fade in, long holds.
6. Foley: add or emphasise real sounds at -6 to -12 dB relative to music.
7. Export reels at 2160x3840 if footage allows (grain and detail survive compression better).

## Quality bar

Nothing jarring: no sudden loud sounds, no flashing, no hard colour shifts between shots. Lowercase consistent. Text never crowds the frame.
